3. IPIP
The International Personality Item Pool states that its items and scales are public domain and may be copied, edited, translated, or used for any purpose without permission or fee. LUMEN-V includes only a small, original non-diagnostic work-style block inspired by public-domain personality measurement logic. It is not a clinical or validated personality test.

4. ESCO
ESCO is a European classification of skills, competences, qualifications and occupations. This implementation does not embed ESCO datasets. Future expansions may connect to ESCO downloads or APIs, observing the applicable licence and attribution notices.

5. Spanish official educational sources
TodoFP, INCUAL and QEDU are used as official reference sources for Spanish educational and occupational guidance. The current implementation includes an orientative matrix and does not claim to be a complete or official catalogue.

6. Proprietary tests
EXPLORA-R, SDS, Strong Interest Inventory and other professional instruments are proprietary or commercial tools subject to their own conditions. LUMEN-V does not copy their items, scoring keys, reports, norms or manuals.
